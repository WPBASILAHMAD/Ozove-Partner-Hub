## THE BOTTLENECK

**Eyebrow:** The bottleneck

**Headline:** Sales isn't your problem. Delivery is.

**Lead:** You can close more than you can ship. So growth quietly caps out — not at your pipeline, but at your production. There are only three ways out, and two of them are traps.

**Card 1 — Hire more headcount:** Recruiting eats three months and might still miss. Every hire is fixed cost whether the projects show up or not — and until they ramp, you're the one filling the gap.

**Card 2 — Juggle freelancers:** Missed deadlines. Inconsistent quality. You spend more time managing five vendors than shipping — and when one flakes, your client is the one who pays for it.

**Card 3 — Become the bottleneck:** Reviewing, QA-ing, gap-filling at midnight. Every hour on execution is an hour not spent selling. Your growth gets capped at your own bandwidth — the most expensive constraint in the building.

**Answer bar:** The fix isn't more headcount, and it isn't a pile of AI tools you have to babysit. It's a production system — senior talent working inside AI workflows, managed for you, ready the day you win the deal.

## Notes before you hand this off

1. **Every number and testimonial is a placeholder.** The 4.9/120+, the metrics, and all three quotes are scaffolding. Publishing invented proof is both a legal and a trust liability — and it's the one section that actually closes. Replace with real figures before launch.

2. **Your strongest asset isn't on the page.** Client switchers are the most persuasive proof you can put in front of a Growmodo-aware buyer. One sentence and a logo from each does more than any headline I can write.

3. **The copy assumes a "capacity audit" funnel and a PM-led delivery model.** If your actual offer mechanics differ — pricing tiers, turnaround guarantees, what the PM layer really does — those lines need to match reality, because that's where the true differentiation lives and generic copy can't fake it.
